Technical scope

Built for demanding ground.

Integrated highway maintenance and earth-retention solutions for complex transport corridors.

01 / Highway maintenance

Keep the corridor moving.

Routine upkeep, rehabilitation and safety upgrades across road networks.

Routine & Periodic Maintenance

Pothole patching, crack sealing, shoulder dressing and drainage cleaning.

Pavement Resurfacing

Bituminous Concrete (BC), Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) and micro-surfacing.

Road Furniture & Safety

Crash barriers, thermoplastic road markings, road studs and reflective signage.

Emergency Restoration

Rapid deployment for monsoon damage, washouts and landslide debris clearance.

02 / Slope protection

Engineer stability into the terrain.

Earth retention and soil reinforcement tailored to site conditions.

Reinforced Earth (RE) Panels

Vertical and near-vertical retaining structures for flyover approaches, bridge abutments and highway widening.

Gabion Walls

Flexible, free-draining stone-filled mesh structures that help relieve hydrostatic pressure.

RCC & Masonry Toe Walls

Basal support for hill slopes and embankments, helping resist toe erosion and shallow slips.

Geogrids & Soil Reinforcement

High-tensile polymer grids for basal reinforcement, slope stabilization and subgrade improvement.

Quality, safety & compliance

Specification is the baseline.

Strict Adherence to Standards
